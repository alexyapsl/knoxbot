3.2 Temperature
When the LLM generates responses, it influences creativity and coherence.
The value ranges from 0 to 1, where values closer to 0 indicating more factual responses, and closer to 
1 indicating more creative responses.
=== page 78 ===
Setting details and related app card - Top-p
 63
For AI bots focused on providing information-based responses, a value around 0.3 is recommended 
generally.
3.3 Top-p
When the LLM generates responses, it adjusts the selection of the next word candidate within a 
cumulative probability range.
Through this, the quality of responses, coherence, and diversity can be balanced appropriately.
The value ranges from 0 to 1, when values closer to 0 indicating more consistent responses, and closer 
to 1 indicating more diverse responses.
For natural and varied expressions, a value around 0.8 is recommended.
3.4 Advanced search
The advanced search feature allows for the application of both keyword search and semantic 
analysis, enabling a more accurate understanding of the user's query intent and providing highly 
relevant responses.
The weight Alpha (a) between keyword search and semantic search can be adjusted to set the 
response preference.
As it approaches 0, responses focus more on exact matches with the entered keywords, while as it 
approaches 1, responses focus more on the context and meaning of the question.
=== page 79 ===
64
 Setting details and related app card - Prompt
For information-based AI bots, a value of around 0.4 is recommended to balance accuracy and 
semantic understanding.
3.5 Rerank Score
Reranking is the process of finely evaluating the relevance of retrieved response candidates and 
adjusting their rankings. 
The Rerank Score is the metric used in this process, where a higher score indicates greater relevance. 
In other words, as the Score increases, responses are generated by referencing only more accurate 
matches, but this may limit the diversity of answers provided.

Appendix C.Developing a Workflow
 Developing a Workflow - About Workflow
C.1 About Workflow
In Workflow, users without development knowledge can easily create business workflows by
selecting and arranging the components of their choice (text, app cards, APIs, and condition
comparison) in a canvas and linking each component with lines.
In Bot Builder, you can use previously created app cards as templates or create layouts by using the
drag-and-drop feature of the Adaptive Card Designer and integrate app card ↔ app card/API in
Workflow to design your business workflow so that is integrated with the internal system.
Complete the following steps to access Workflow.
 
1. Click the My Bot tab on the top of the Bot Builder home screen. 
2. Select a bot from the bot list on the "My Bot" screen. 
3. Click Workflow on the bot details screen. 
4. Click the Register button.
